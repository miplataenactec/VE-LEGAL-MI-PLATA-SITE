Your financial data never leaves your phone.

The transactions you record, your wallets, your debts, your categories and the receipts you attach live in a database inside the application, on your device. We do not upload them to any server, we do not back them up to the cloud, and we do not share them with anyone. No copy of your books exists on our side.

What does leave the phone is the minimum needed for the app to work: fetching exchange rates and checking whether your subscription is active. The exact detail follows.

What stays on your device

Never transmitted:

  • Transactions: amounts, dates, categories, descriptions and the BCV rate they were recorded at.
  • Wallets: names, types and balances, which are derived from your transactions.
  • Debts: the names of people you owe or who owe you, and the amounts.
  • Recurring payments and their rules.
  • Custom categories you have created.
  • Attached receipts: photos and files you associate with a transaction are copied into private application storage that other apps cannot reach.
  • Your preferences: theme, language and biometric lock.

Uninstalling the application makes Android delete all of it. Since we hold no copy, that deletion is final. If you want to keep your information, use the export feature before uninstalling.

What is transmitted, and why

Exchange rates

The app queries our server for BCV and Binance P2P rates. That request contains no data about you: it asks for the day's rates, not for information about you.

Subscription check

To determine whether your Premium subscription is active, the app sends our server:

DataWhat it is
Device identifierOn Android, the ANDROID_ID assigned by the system. It identifies the device, not you: it contains no name, email or phone number.
Anonymous session identifierA code generated by Firebase without asking you for any personal data.
Purchase receiptWhen you buy, the receipt issued by Google Play, so it can be verified against Google.

From that we keep a record that this device has an active subscription and until when. It is not tied to an identity: we never ask you for an email, phone number, name or identity document.

About the device identifier, plainly: ANDROID_ID is stable and survives uninstalling the app. It only resets on a factory reset. It works that way on purpose, so you do not lose your subscription if you reinstall.

Authenticity verification

Every request to our server carries a Google check (Play Integrity) confirming it comes from a genuine install of Mi Plata rather than from a program impersonating it. It protects the service against abuse and collects no information about you.

Advertising

Premium users see no ads at all. For them, this section does not apply.

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. As the provider, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier and data about your interaction with the ads, under its own policies.

We hand Google none of your financial data. AdMob has no access to your transactions, balances or debts: it operates in a separate space within the application.

You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings, under Google → Ads, or remove ads entirely by subscribing to Premium.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

About the location permission

On Android 11 and earlier, the system required the location permission in order to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices, even when the application had no interest in where you are. That is why it appears declared.

Mi Plata does not determine, store or transmit your location. On Android 12 and later that permission is no longer requested, because the system allows explicitly declaring that Bluetooth scanning is not used to infer location — which is exactly our case.

What we do not do

  • We use no analytics. The application embeds no tool that records your behaviour, the screens you open or the time you spend on them.
  • We do not sell or transfer data to third parties.
  • We build no profiles of your spending habits.
  • We do not ask you for an email, phone number, name, identity document or banking details.
  • There is no cloud backup. That is a design decision, not a gap: we cannot leak what we never held.

Sharing information: you decide

The export feature generates a CSV or PDF file with your transactions and hands it to the Android share menu. From there the destination is yours to choose: email, messaging, or saving it on the phone.

That file contains your financial information. Once you share it, it is governed by the terms of whichever application you send it to, not by this policy.

Children

Mi Plata is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Since we collect no data that identifies you, there is no profile of yours on our servers to consult, correct or export.

  • Delete your financial data: uninstall the application, or clear its data from Android settings. Being local, it disappears with you.
  • Delete your subscription record: write to miplata.enact@gmail.com stating that you want the record associated with your device removed. Note that this also removes your entitlement to an active subscription.
  • Manage or cancel the subscription: from Google Play → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Google under its own terms.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this document and the date in the header. If the change is significant, we will announce it inside the application before it takes effect.
